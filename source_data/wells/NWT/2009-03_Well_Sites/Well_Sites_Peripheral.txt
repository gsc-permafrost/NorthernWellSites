
Well_Sites_Peripheral.txt
March 2009

Description: Point shapefile of the oil and gas well sites, monitored by the National Energy Board (NEB), located within 100km of the Peel Plateau & Plain sedimentary basin boundaries. A few additional well sites outside of this buffer distance have also been included, as they are relevant to regional cross-sections. 
Please contact the Frontier Information Office of the NEB for more information and to obtain the latest well data set; http://www.neb-one.gc.ca/clf-nsi/rthnb/nrthffshr/frntrnfrmtn/frntrnfrmtnffc-eng.html

Projection and Datum: Universal Transverse Mercator Zone 9, NAD 83.

Attributes:
WID - NEB well identification
WELL_NAME - Name of well
OPERATOR - Operator name
UWI - Unique Well Identifier
STATUS - Current well status
CLASSIFICA - Classification of well
SPUD_DATE - Spud Date
RIG_RELEAS - Rig release date
LAT - Latitude, in decimal degrees
LONG - Longitude, in decimal degrees
DATUM - Datum of coordinates
EASTING - Easting, in metres
NORTHING - Northing, in metres
ZONE - UTM zone of coordinates
SOURCE - Source of data
REFERENCE - Reference to this digital atlas

Processing information:	
Well site data was received from NEB, Apr. 2009. Spreadsheet was imported into ArcGIS, the resulting shapefile was reprojected to UTM Zone 9 and wells within a 100km buffer around the Peel Plateau & Plain region were selected. 

Source: 
National Energy Board, Apr. 2009.
